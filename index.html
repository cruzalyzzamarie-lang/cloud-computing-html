What is Cloud Computing?

Cloud Computing is a on-demand delivery of computing services which can serve to provide or offer data storage, servers, databases, networking, and even software through the internet with a pay-as-you-go pricing model.

cloud computing Services

  • Zoom

    • Zoom is a cloud-based software platform for audio and video conferencing that records meetings and saves them to the cloud so users can have access to them anytime, anywhere.

  • Dropbox

    • Dropbox facilitates collaboration by allowing users to share large files.

  • Finix

    • Finix is a payment processing platform that helps software companies, independent vendors and marketplaces to embed payments infrastructure directly into their applications.
